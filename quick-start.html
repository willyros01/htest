FIDUNIO

Private Messaging

Welcome to FIDUNIO, an invitation-only private messaging app for one-to-one and group conversations. This short guide explains what to expect before you join.

Before you join

Your invitation is personal, single-use, and expires on the date shown in the invitation message. You can read this guide without creating an account.

Keep your invitation link private. Whoever successfully uses a valid invitation first consumes it.

Getting started

  1. Open the Join FIDUNIO link in your invitation.
  2. Verify the invitation and review the role assigned to you.
  3. Enter your display name, email address, and a password to create your account.
  4. After account creation, FIDUNIO opens directly. You do not need to unlock again immediately after signing in.
  5. Set up the local PIN/biometric unlock option when available for quicker protection when returning to the app.

Privacy & security

FIDUNIO is designed for private messaging with encrypted message transport/storage features and per-device identity. Your FIDUNIO account and each registered device are separate security layers. Never share your password, PIN, or invitation link.

Administrators can manage account access, including suspension and expiration, but should not need your password.

Messaging basics

After joining, use Messages to open or start a private conversation. Delivery/read status appears with outgoing messages. Group conversations are being added through the same private FIDUNIO environment.

Using another device

Each browser/device receives its own FIDUNIO device identity. Do not clear browser/site data on a working installation unless you intentionally want to reset that local installation.

Ready to join?

Return to the invitation message you received and tap its Join FIDUNIO link. If the invitation has expired or was already used, ask the person who invited you for a new invitation.
